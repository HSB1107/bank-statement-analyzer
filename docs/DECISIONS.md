# Decisions & deviations

Short log of choices that are not dictated by the PDF, or where screens disagree.

## Stack
- **Next.js 16.4 / React 19.3 / Tailwind v4** — current versions from `create-next-app`. Tailwind runs through the `@tailwindcss/turbopack` loader that the template generated, so there is no PostCSS config.
- **TanStack Table pinned to v8**: npm `latest` is v9, a new major with a changed API. v8 is stable and well documented.
- **`radix-ui` (unified package) + `cmdk`** are the primitives shadcn/ui is built on. Components are written shadcn-style in `src/shared/ui`, not generated by the CLI.
- **`@tanstack/react-virtual`** virtualises the 9,318-row table (required by the brief; TanStack Table has no built-in virtualiser).
- No `eslint-plugin-boundaries`: feature isolation is enforced with `no-restricted-imports` patterns (deep `@/features/*/…` imports are banned; `shared/` cannot import features), so no extra dependency.
- Unchecked casts are banned (`consistent-type-assertions: never`). The DI registry stays type-safe by giving each token its own typed `WeakMap`.
- `cacheComponents`/`partialPrefetching` from the template are off: the app is client-data driven (TanStack Query over service ports).
- `PageProps`/`LayoutProps` globals need `next typegen`; routes use explicit prop types so `tsc` works standalone.

## Data model & generation
- **Seeded scenario generators.** Each customer's ledger is generated once from a fixed seed (mulberry32) in `src/shared/data/generators`. Scenario events from the PDF (salary dates, EMIs, the 05-May ACH return, the K S Trading round trip, the missing April SBI statement) are scripted. Discretionary spend and the bulk corporate rows come from the PRNG. Closed-form calibration steps (documented inline) pin balance averages and anchors. Every screen figure is recomputed from the transactions by pure selectors in `src/shared/data/selectors`, and invariant tests assert the PDF values.
- **Arjun — expense-to-income 93.5%** is the **mean of the six monthly ratios** (expenses exclude the SIP and the ₹2.40 L one-off). The ratio of averages (₹1,41,048 / ₹1,50,392) would be 93.8%; only the mean of monthly ratios also reproduces "Jan 81.7% → Jun 100.4%".
- **Arjun — the returned ACH** on 05-May is kept as a statement row with `ledgerEffect: excluded/returned_unpaid`. It never moves balances or totals; the ₹590 charge is a posted row.
- **Scoring models are config.** SCM-RTL v2.1, LQ-RTL v1.4, IS-RTL v1.2 and SCM-CORP v1.3 live in `src/shared/data/scoring` as rule tables. The PDF gives dimension scores but not the rules, so the rule constants were chosen to reproduce them from the computed metrics. **SCM-CORP weights are not in the PDF**: OCF 0.20, Liquidity 0.15, Collections 0.20, Debt 0.15, Concentration 0.15, Integrity 0.15 → 61.3 → 61. Previous-period (Jul–Dec 25) scores are stored assessment records.
- **Meridian — own transfers by month.** The H1 total (₹62.20 cr, hence gross credits ₹223.80 cr) is exact. The June OD stress (71% peak, ₹0.31 cr minimum bank balance, nine sub-₹0.50 cr days) needs more treasury movement than screen 11's June bar implies. June therefore carries more own-transfer volume and Jan–May proportionally less. Monthly *collections*, *net operating* and all buckets in the cash-flow table are exact.
- **Meridian — April SBI.** No SBI rows exist for April. The May statement opens at the bank's true balance (₹1.24 cr drawn), so `Opening + Credits − Debits = Closing` is flagged for SBI. The OD transfers in April appear on ICICI only, as **6 unmatched legs** (the 486 matched pairs are exact). April SBI balances and utilisation are `NotAvailable` everywhere.
- **Meridian — Precision Castings in April** is paid from HDFC (its SBI side would otherwise be unobservable). The early-June Shakti and 45% of June supplier payments are paid from the OD, so the OD climbs through debits rather than transfers.
- **Per-account extraction confidence.** The PDF shows ICICI 99.6%, HDFC 99.9%, SBI 94.1% and an overall 98.9%. Those can't both hold on a row-weighted basis. The overall 98.9% (shown on four screens) is kept, so SBI is **93.2%**.
- **Dashboard work queue** shows open cases from the pipeline (46+61+29+52+18+87 = 293); the PDF's "8 of 247" does not reconcile with the pipeline counts. 336 cases (2,947 − 2,611) are archived without a profile and sit outside the pipeline.
- **Awaiting Review delta**: 87 vs 77 a month earlier is +13.0%; the PDF shows +12.9%.
- **Meridian collections weeks** are 7-day blocks from 01-Jan (181 days → 26 blocks).
